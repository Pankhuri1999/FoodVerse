How to play

This is a seated kitchen. Press Enter VR. On a computer, keep the preview on Controllers and hold Trigger · Grab on the Left or Right Touch pad. On a Meta headset, squeeze the real trigger. Food sits on the trays until you grab it. Let go in the air and it floats.

Computer: hold Trigger to grab · Q left grab · click right grab · H heat · C cool · S stir · G stabilizer

Recipes

Current recipe

Ember Spark — Cosmic Fruit and Star Pepper. Requires heat.

  1. Pick up Cosmic Fruit and Star Pepper. Place both in the mixer bowl.
  2. Press Heat (H). If food floats, catch it or press Stabilizer (G).
  3. Press S, or hold the pointer on the bowl, until stir reaches 45%.
  4. Pick up Ember Dragon onto the Ice Gate, then place the cookie wafer on Cookie Bridge.
