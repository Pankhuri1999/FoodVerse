# Phase 6 — Creature abilities

Deliverable: three creatures with usable abilities that change the world.

## Run

```text
hand_tracking\run_phase6.bat
```

## Goal

Solve all 3 challenges:

| Creature | Ability | Challenge |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| Ember Dragon | Melt | Frozen Gate on the bridge |
| Grove Guardian | Grow | Barren Soil |
| Mint Current | Freeze Path | Open Water |

## How to play

1. Pinch a creature from the bottom tray (or press **1 / 2 / 3**)
2. Drag it onto its matching challenge until you see a glowing ring / “RELEASE to activate”
3. **Release the pinch** — the ability fires automatically
4. Backup: press **A** or **Space** while near the challenge
5. Repeat until `Challenges solved: 3/3`

Matches:
- Ember → Frozen Gate (center)
- Grove → Barren Soil (left)
- Mint → Open Water (right)

**R** reset · **Q** quit

## Design note

This is the “life transforms worlds” beat: cooking created the creatures (Phase 4); now their abilities restore the Cookie Kingdom (sets up Phase 7’s full rescue mission).
